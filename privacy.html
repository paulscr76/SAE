Privacy and local data

Your information stays under your control.

Version 5.0 does not include a public customer account or website database.

Checking this browser…

Household Command Centre

The Command Centre stores answers in the browser’s local storage so progress can continue on the same device. The information is not automatically sent to Paul, Vercel, GitHub or a customer database. Use “Clear saved plan” to remove it.

Do not enter names, addresses, account numbers, payment details or other identifying information into the public tools.

Bill-reading helper

Selected images or PDFs are displayed locally in the browser tab using a temporary object URL. The document is not uploaded by this website and is not saved into local storage. Closing or refreshing the page removes the preview.

Professional reports

The Report Studio creates print-ready A4 pages in the browser. Printing or saving as PDF uses the browser’s print system. Reports are not automatically uploaded or sent to Paul.

Review a report before saving or sharing it because it may contain household figures, action items and selected meeting-brief information.

Energy calculator

The calculator runs in the browser. Results can be saved to the Command Centre on the same device, printed or shared only when the visitor chooses an action.

Calendly, WhatsApp and other services

Calendly, WhatsApp, telephone, email and Linktree are third-party services. They handle information under their own privacy terms when a visitor chooses to use them.

Analytics-ready events

The package includes anonymous interaction labels for future Vercel Analytics integration. Household answers, bill figures and calculator values are not included in those events. Analytics does not become active merely because the labels exist; the feature must be enabled and integrated in Vercel.

Private adviser dashboard

The private adviser platform is deliberately separate and is not included in the public website or GitHub files. Real customer information must never be placed in this public repository.
