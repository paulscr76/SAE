YOUR INFORMATION

How the public website and calculator handle information.

Home / Privacy
In plain English: the public calculator runs in your browser. It does not ask for your identity and does not send entries to a website database.

Energy usage calculator

The calculator uses the property and household details entered in the browser to create an indicative result. The website does not automatically send those entries to Paul or save them in a customer database.

Choosing a WhatsApp button creates a prepared message containing the result. Nothing is sent until the visitor chooses to continue in WhatsApp.

Contacting Paul

WhatsApp, Calendly, telephone, email and Linktree are third-party services. Information shared through those services is handled under their respective privacy terms.

Cookies and analytics

This Version 2.0 package does not set advertising cookies. Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights can be enabled separately in the Vercel dashboard. The website owner should review the applicable Vercel privacy documentation before enabling either service.

Offline website files

A small service worker may store public website assets on the visitor’s device so pages load more reliably. It does not store customer account information.

Contact

Questions about this website can be sent to paul.scrase@uw.partners.
