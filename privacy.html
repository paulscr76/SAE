Privacy and local data

Your information stays under your control.

The planning tools save information locally in this browser so you remain in control of what is shared.

Local data check

See what this device is storing.

Checking this browser…

Household plan

The Command Centre stores answers in the browser’s local storage so progress can continue on the same device. The information is not automatically sent to Paul or to a customer database. Use “Clear saved plan” to remove it.

Do not enter names, addresses, account numbers, payment details or other identifying information into the public tools.

Bill-reading helper

Selected images or PDFs are displayed locally in the browser tab using a temporary object URL. The document is not uploaded by this website and is not saved into local storage. Closing or refreshing the page removes the preview.

Printable summaries

The printable-summary tool creates formatted A4 pages in the browser. Printing or saving as PDF uses the browser’s print system. Summaries are not automatically uploaded or sent to Paul.

Review a summary before saving or sharing it because it may contain household figures, action items and selected meeting-brief information.

Energy calculator

The calculator runs in the browser. Results can be saved to the Command Centre on the same device, printed or shared only when the visitor chooses an action.

Calendly, WhatsApp and other services

Calendly, WhatsApp, telephone, email and Linktree are third-party services. They handle information under their own privacy terms when a visitor chooses to use them.

Website usage information

The website may record basic page and button activity to understand which public features are useful. Household answers, bill figures and calculator values are not included in those interaction labels.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to paul.scrase@uw.partners.
