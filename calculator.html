USE THE STRONGEST ENERGY INFORMATION YOU HAVE

Start with actual annual kWh where possible. Add official property information, meter readings or a transparent household estimate—each clearly labelled by source and confidence.

Actual household usage is not normally available through an open internet search.

Official property records can improve the picture, but real consumption must come from a bill, supplier export, authorised smart-meter service or meter readings supplied by the household.

Step 1

Choose the best available source

Official property information

Find an Energy Performance Certificate

Available for England and Wales when the official data connection is configured. An EPC describes the building and uses standard assumptions; it is not an actual meter record.

Checking official property-data connection…

Add household context

EPC information describes the building. Occupancy and lifestyle information helps create a separate, clearly labelled estimate.

Customer-supplied actual usage

Enter annual kWh from a bill or supplier

Look for “annual consumption”, “estimated annual usage” or a twelve-month total. Record whether the supplier figure is actual or estimated.

Open the Bill Helper
Meter-reading annualisation

Estimate a year from two readings

This uses the consumption between two dates and scales it to 365 days. Seasonal heating means a short period can be misleading, so longer periods are better.

Use the value on the bill where available.
Transparent household estimate

Estimate from the home and household

Step 2

Choose the public benchmark

Benchmarks use 2024 domestic medians from the government’s National Energy Efficiency Data-Framework 2026 release. They describe typical groups, not this individual household.

Optional scenario

What could change?

After creating a current result, model broad changes to heating, occupancy, solar or an electric vehicle. The scenario is indicative and does not calculate prices or guaranteed savings.

Source labels used by this tool

A

Actual annual usage

From a bill, supplier export or authorised data connection. Strongest basis when the period and reading quality are clear.

B

Meter annualisation

Calculated from readings supplied by the household. Longer periods reduce seasonal distortion.

C

Official property model

EPC building information combined with household context. Useful, but not actual consumption.

D

Household estimate

A transparent planning model based on property type, heating, occupancy and selected changes.
