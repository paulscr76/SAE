01How to find annual energy usageAnnual kWh, meter readings and bill costs explained+

Look for annual consumption

Electricity and gas usage are usually shown separately in kilowatt-hours, written as kWh. Do not use the amount of money owed, the annual cost estimate or the number currently displayed on the meter.

When the figure is unavailable, use the household calculator as an indicative starting point.

02What to check before reviewing broadbandSpeed, reliability, contract and household use+

Prepare four facts

Note your current provider, the speed you normally receive, where the connection struggles and whether you are still in contract. A household that works from home or has several simultaneous streams may value reliability and capacity more than the cheapest headline price.

03Planning a household mobile reviewCoverage, data, contract timing and family lines+

Start with real use

Record how many lines are involved, where signal matters, typical monthly data use and upgrade dates. Moving several lines together can be a different decision from changing a single phone.

04Solar and electric vehiclesWhy they change the energy picture+

Imported electricity is not the whole story

Solar can reduce electricity imported from the grid, while an EV can add substantial charging use. The calculator treats these as broad adjustments, but actual annual kWh remains the better source.

Test a scenario →
05Heating type and energy usageGas boilers, electric heating and heat pumps+

Compare the correct fuels

A gas-heated household normally uses considerably more gas kWh than electricity kWh. Electric heating and heat pumps move more of the household demand into electricity, so comparing electricity alone without knowing the heating type can be misleading.

Ask for the complete picture
