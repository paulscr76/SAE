BILL-READING HELPER

Open a bill or statement locally, identify the useful figures and save them into the Command Centre. This version does not upload or automatically read the document.

Step 1

Images and PDFs are displayed only in this browser tab.

Choose an energy bill or annual statement

Accepted for local preview: JPG, PNG, WebP or PDF.

For privacy, close this page when finished. The file itself is not saved into the website’s local storage.
Step 2

Look for these labels

Annual electricity consumptionUsually shown in kWh. Do not enter the account balance, bill cost or meter reading.
Annual gas consumptionGas annual usage is also shown in kWh, even though the meter may record cubic metres.
Unit rateOften shown as pence per kWh. This is not the annual usage figure.
Standing chargeUsually pence per day. This is separate from usage.
Tariff or contract end dateUseful for a conversation, but not needed for the calculator.

What this tool does not do

No automatic extraction yet.

This release helps you find and record the figures but does not use OCR or artificial intelligence to read the document. That avoids sending sensitive documents to an external service.

A future secure version could add confirmed, consent-based extraction.
