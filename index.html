Helpful guides

Useful answers before you make any decision.

Practical information, without the hard sell.

01Find your annual energy usageWhat to look for on a bill or statement+

Look for figures labelled annual consumption, estimated annual consumption, or usage in kWh. Electricity and gas normally appear separately. Avoid entering your bill cost, account balance or the number shown on the meter itself.

Open the energy calculator →
02Check broadband before changingSpeed, reliability and contract timing+

Note your current provider, typical download speed, contract end date, any exit charge and where the connection struggles. The cheapest headline price is not always the best household fit.

Add broadband to my review →
03Think clearly about flexible earningQuestions worth asking first+

Ask what activity is involved, how training works, what costs may apply, how much time you can realistically give, and how income is generated. Income is never guaranteed and should not be presented as automatic.
