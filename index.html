Common questions

What people usually want to know.

Is this a comparison website?

No. It does not rank suppliers or present comparison tables. Paul helps you explore whether eligible Utility Warehouse services may suit your household.

Do I have to change every service?

No. You can discuss the eligible services that matter to you and decide what, if anything, you wish to proceed with.

Will I definitely save money?

Your result depends on your circumstances, usage, eligibility and current terms. Paul will explain the available options without promising a personal outcome.

Is the earning opportunity compulsory?

No. It is completely optional and separate from discussing household services. Income is not guaranteed.
